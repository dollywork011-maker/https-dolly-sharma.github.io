Recruitment Funnel Analysis — FY25

People Analytics Looker Studio Recruitment HR Dashboard

A full-cycle recruitment analytics dashboard built in Looker Studio, analysing 1,200+ applications across 8 departments for FY25. The dashboard tracks every stage of the hiring funnel — from application to joining — and surfaces insights on time to hire, source effectiveness, offer acceptance rates, rejection patterns, and gender diversity. Designed to support data-driven hiring decisions and identify process bottlenecks before they affect delivery.
